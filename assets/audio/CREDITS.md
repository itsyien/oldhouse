The MP3s in `sources.json` are originals supplied by the user. BackgroundMusic loops underneath the room tone. JumpScare plays for Naiwa, Gunmu, Sun, Yujie and Liang. NaiWaSpawn plays at entrance. RushReturner plays for Wang Jingwei's pass and Tenno's first pass. RushReturnerWarning plays at the start of each warning. Hirohito2 plays on Tenno's return pass. JumpScareWANGHIROHITO plays from the start of the black screen when either sweeper catches the player. YuJieBackgroundSFX loops while Yujie is present.

Pause settings offer a persistent background audio switch and a separate master volume. Warnings, impacts and interaction sounds continue when background audio is off.

StoneSpeakAudio.mp3 is the user-supplied guide stone recording. It plays from the stone and temporarily takes the place of the ambient music.
